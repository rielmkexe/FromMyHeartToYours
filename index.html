From My Heart to Yours

To my dearest, Erin

I couldn’t sleep properly last night. I know you’re still hurting deep inside after what happened, and I want to apologize again for everything I’ve done that caused you pain. I know I can’t undo the past or immediately erase the pain and doubts, but I am truly, deeply sorry.

I know bago pa lang tayo, and yet I’ve already hurt you. I’m so sorry for every time I’ve disappointed you, and for all the things you’re still keeping to yourself right now. Thank you for always being so understanding and loving. Please know that I will never take your love and kindness for granted.

I know saying “sorry” won’t magically fix things or erase the fear in your heart, but I hope you know this apology comes from the absolute deepest part of me. I completely understand if you’re still hurting or doubting me... I’m so sorry for triggering your fears. I know I can’t change what happened, but I can learn how to love and hold you the way your heart truly needs. You deserve the world, babu. You are so precious to me, and hurting you is the absolute last thing I ever wanted to do.

I love you so much. If only you knew how much better my life became the moment you entered it. I’m so thankful the universe let me meet and love someone like you. Moving forward, I will stay consistent. I will prove to you that my love is real and unconditional.

So please, let me keep learning about you. Let me learn how to hold your heart, and let me love you the way you deserve to be loved.

Sincerely loving you,
Gab
